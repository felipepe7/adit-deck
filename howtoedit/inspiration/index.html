The rule

Inspiration → Translate → Apply

We don't copy reels. We study why a reel makes you feel something in the first two seconds, then we rebuild that exact feeling with our client's footage, our client's brand and audio that fits their industry. A fitness brand, a dentist, a drywall contractor and a wealth advisor all get the same cinematic energy, just translated.

01

Shot selection

The cinematic shot formula

Every HRLVL reel follows the same emotional arc. Build every inspiration-style reel in this order, whatever the industry.

Mood

Silhouette, low light, texture (rain, steam, dust). No face, no logo yet.

The grind

The hard part of the job: effort, sweat, hands, tools, early mornings.

The human

First real face close-up, eyes and breath. This is where the beat lands.

Motion

Tracking shot, walking, driving, running. Momentum for the middle.

The payoff

Result, reveal, finished work, happy client, confidence.

The brand

Logo, product, offer or date on black. Sound drops out.

CAMERA

Shallow depth of field, slow motion on texture shots (shoot 60fps or higher), tight crops, slow push-ins. Handheld micro-movement is fine; shaky is not.

LIGHT

Golden hour, night with one practical light, or a hard window light. Backlight and flare make anyone look cinematic.

GRADE

Pick one look per reel: monochrome, warm golden or muted with one accent color. Crushed blacks, no oversaturation.

The most important part

Sound engineering + audio

Viewers forgive an average shot but they scroll on bad audio. The audio is what creates the emotion, sparks the hype and makes people replay. Notice that HRLVL names its sounds after its own campaigns ("BE#1", "TAKECONTROL", "PURSUIT", "HRLVL"). Their audio is part of the brand.

LayerWhat it doesHow we build it
1 · Voice or quoteCarries the message and the emotionOne line, 12 words max. Client's own voice first, a pro voiceover second. Clean it, compress it, and keep it the loudest thing in the mix.
2 · Music bedSets the mood: grit, hype, calm or luxuryLicensed only (Artlist or Epidemic for ads, Instagram's library for organic). Dark cinematic or ambient for grind, driving for hype, warm piano or strings for heart. Duck it under the voice.
3 · Sound designMakes every cut feel physicalRisers into the drop, whooshes on transitions, a low impact or sub hit on the reveal, reverse swells before the quote.
4 · FoleyMakes it real and personalRecorded or library sounds of the actual action: breathing, footsteps, water, tools, plates, keys, the drill. Foley is what sells a slow-motion shot.
5 · SilenceCreates tension and makes the payoff hitDrop everything for a half beat before the key word or reveal. End on silence or a single hit under the logo card.

CUT ON THE BEAT

Hard cuts on kicks and snares, slow-motion on the swells. The biggest visual lands on the drop, about two thirds of the way through.

MIX FOR PHONES

Most people watch on phone speakers. Check the mix on your phone before you export: the voice clear, no muddy bass, no clipping.

LOOP IT

Under 15 seconds? End on the same sound you start on so the replay feels seamless. Replays push reach.

Rights: we study these reels for structure and feel. We never rip another creator's audio or footage into a client reel. Rebuild the sound with licensed music and our own recordings.

Quote text

Branded quote and inspirational text

SMALL AND SPACED

Small, all caps and widely letter-spaced, centered. It looks premium because it doesn't shout.

ONE WORD PER CUT

For voice-led reels, change the word on every cut and sync it to the spoken word.

CLIENT BRAND ONLY

Client fonts and colors from their brand guidelines. One accent color for the key word. Logo on the end card.

WRITE ORIGINAL QUOTES

Short, true to the client's world, no clichés. Pull lines from their own interviews when we have them: their words land hardest.

Before you export

Inspiration edit checklist

  • I watched the inspiration reel 5 times with sound on and wrote down its hook, shot order, text style and every sound layer
  • The first 3 seconds are mood: texture or silhouette, no logo, no talking head
  • The music is licensed and matches the client's world (grit, hype, calm or luxury)
  • Voice or quote is the loudest layer, with music ducked underneath
  • Sound design is in: riser, whooshes, an impact on the reveal and real foley
  • Cuts land on the beat, and the biggest shot lands on the drop
  • Quote text is small, spaced, centered and in the client's brand font and colors
  • One grade for the whole reel (monochrome, golden or muted with one accent)
  • Ends on the client's logo or offer card, with silence or a single hit under it
  • I checked the mix on my phone speaker before posting it for review
