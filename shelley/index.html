About Shelley

She has held the roles she trains

Align My Practice is Shelley's third business. It is a boutique consulting firm built on real orthodontic practice experience, working alongside doctors as an extension of their leadership.

  • More than 30 years in the dental and orthodontic industry, in every role within a practice
  • Works mainly with private orthodontic practices, and with pediatric and general dental offices adding orthodontics, clear aligners or airway-focused treatment
  • Seven role-specific Master Classes, from treatment and scheduling coordinators to clinical team leaders, each with an SOP playbook and three months of follow-up support
  • No long-term contracts: onsite or virtual training, scheduled à la carte
  • Speaker at the Western Orthodontic Conference (WOCON) 2026
