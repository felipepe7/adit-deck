About Mark

A career built inside dentistry

Mark is a sales and marketing leader who has built global strategies, grown new business and led consultative sales teams for medium and large dental companies. He has set up direct operations in Australia, Canada and the UK, and has worked every side of the industry: manufacturers, distribution and selling straight to the practice.

  • Director of Business Development at Media NV, helping podiatry, dental and specialty practices grow
  • Former Executive Vice President of Global Sales, Vista Apex Dental Products
  • Former Director of Corporate Accounts, Medicom Group: added $4M in private label business in 2021 and managed accounts like Patterson Dental
  • Regional Manager at Benco Dental for greater Chicagoland and Southeastern Wisconsin, then Key Account Manager at Zyris Isolite
  • University of Wisconsin-Whitewater
