Laurie helps dental leaders build the mindset, systems and room to grow. Her belief is simple: leaders develop first, so their teams can grow. She speaks to practice owners, office managers and clinicians about leadership, team culture and the systems that keep a practice running without burning out the people who run it.

About Laurie

Leadership from inside the practice

Laurie has worked in dentistry since 2011 and runs the practice at Smile 4 Me Dental in Clermont, Florida. She writes for AADOM, the American Association of Dental Office Management, and speaks on leadership, team culture and practice operations.

  • Dental Practice Manager at Smile 4 Me Dental, Clermont, Florida
  • AADOM Diplomate with High Distinction (DAADOM), awarded at AADOM 2026
  • Strategic Leadership certification through Wharton
  • Author for AADOM's Dental Managers blog: leadership style, owner and manager alignment, scheduling and professional growth
  • Guest on EG Dominates, Episode 26
  • Featured in DeW Life Magazine, Summer 2026
  • Featured speaker for the Circle of Dental Excellence (C.O.D.E.) Inner Circle series
