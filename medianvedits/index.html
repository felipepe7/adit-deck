The Media NV edit rules

Their brand, the caption style they signed off on, and what they expect after every event. Read this before you open the timeline.

medianv.com
🏢Who they are

A marketing agency for dental, podiatry, med spa, chiro and optometry. Calgary + Seattle. Every video proves results: patients, rankings, leads.

🎨Their colors

Teal #00A7B5 accent, black, charcoal #212121, white. Teal only for keywords, title cards and end cards.

🔤Their type

Akkurat on their site. Use Inter Tight Bold for captions, ExtraBold for titles.

♡Their logo

White heart-check mark + "mediaNV" top-left on talking heads and testimonials. Centered on the end card.

⬛Captions: dark box

White bold text on a dark box. Their #1 revision note last time.

⬇️Captions: lower third

Never in the middle of the frame, never over faces.

AaCaptions: word for word

Synced to the audio, spelled right. Sentence case is fine.

🖥️Horizontal first

16:9 master for YouTube and the homepage, then a 9:16 reframe for Reels and Shorts.

🏷️Title cards

"CLIENT EXPERIENCE" before testimonials. Event name card in teal before recaps.

🎬Hide every jump cut

Booth and floor B-roll over every cut in an interview.

📦What to deliver

Edited, subtitled interviews, all raw B-roll organized, recap and promo reels.

🚫No Puro Flow branding

Media NV deliverables end on their logo only.

Make yours match.

Start with Talking Head 1 for captions and the Taylor × Tokuyama interview for partner videos. Questions go in #medianv-fabian.
