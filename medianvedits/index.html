03 · Caption rules (from their marketing team)

The #1 thing they've sent back for revisions

Media NV's marketing lead asked for these exact changes on the PDC batch. Get them right the first time.

  • ✔ Visible: white bold text on a dark box behind it
  • ✔ Placed in the lower third, not the middle of the frame
  • ✔ Word-for-word, synced to the audio. Spell everything right ("dentists", not "dentist")
  • ✔ Sentence case is fine, doesn't need to be all caps
  • ✔ Highlight 1 key word per line in teal
  • ✘ Centered captions covering faces
  • ✘ Paraphrased or off-beat captions

05 · Signature moves

What their approved videos have in common

OPEN

Location or event title card

Drone or skyline of the host city, or a teal "Pacific Dental Conference" style card with the Media NV mark. "CLIENT EXPERIENCE" title card before testimonials.

MIDDLE

Booth energy

Handshakes, team members walking doctors through dashboards, crowds on the expo floor, speakers on stage. Cut on the beat, keep it moving.

TALKING HEADS

Stable, eye level, booth behind

Medium shot, teal booth wall in the background, logo top-left, keyword captions ("VERIFIED LEADS", "NEW PATIENTS").

TESTIMONIALS

Let the doctor talk

One clean take straight to camera, cut the ums, punch in on the strongest line, B-roll over jump cuts. 45 to 75 seconds.

PARTNER VIDEOS

Two-person, walk-and-talk

Like the Taylor × Tokuyama interview below: both people in frame, quick questions, branded color card inserts, product or workshop B-roll.

CLOSE

Logo end card

Centered heart-check mark, medianv.com, 1.5 to 2 seconds. No Puro Flow branding on Media NV deliverables.
